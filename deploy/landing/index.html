About us

Fire safety you can rely on, from people who know it.

Samruddhi Fire is a fire protection company based in Koparkhairane, Navi Mumbai, founded by Yogesh Bankar. We work on fire prevention, detection, suppression and emergency preparedness, protecting lives and assets on sites of every size.

Our team of trained technicians and engineers handles a system from start to finish: supplying the material, laying the lines, commissioning the system, and keeping it in working order year after year.
